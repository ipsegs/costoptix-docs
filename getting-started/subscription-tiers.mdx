## Add-ons

On self-serve plans you can add cloud accounts, Kubernetes nodes, hosts and AI coding tool users beyond what your plan includes, without moving to a bigger plan. Add-ons are billed monthly per unit. Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://costoptix.com/#pricing) and in **Settings → Billing**.

When you reach a limit, CostOptix tells you which plan or add-on would cover it before anything new is added. Existing sources and users are never removed.

## Questions

**Can I change plans at any time?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

**When do daily API limits reset?**
At 00:00 UTC every day.

**Does history disappear when I downgrade?**
No. Your plan sets how far back you can query, not how long we keep data. Requests older than your plan's window are limited to that window rather than rejected.

**Can Starter, Growth or Professional use SSO?**
No. SSO needs Business or Enterprise.
