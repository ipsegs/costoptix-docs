## What is CostOptix?

CostOptix puts all of your infrastructure spend in one place and counts every dollar once. It connects to your cloud bills, runs small agents on your clusters and machines, and reads usage from AI coding tools. Then it tells you three things:

- **What it costs.** One total across every source, with daily detail down to the resource, namespace, service or session.
- **Who owns it.** Teams claim the spend that is theirs, and each month closes into a sealed bill that does not change later.
- **What to do next.** Anomalies, budgets, daily limits and savings recommendations, with savings checked against the bill after you act.

## What you can connect

| Source | How it connects | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| [Microsoft Azure](/providers/azure) | Read-only service principal | Billed cost per service and resource |
| [Amazon Web Services](/providers/aws) | Read-only IAM user or role | Billed cost per service and resource |
| [Google Cloud](/providers/gcp) | BigQuery billing export | Billed cost per service, resource and label |
| [Kubernetes](/providers/kubernetes) | In-cluster agent | Cost per namespace and workload, with invoice reconciliation |
| [Linux machines](/providers/hosts) | Host agent (systemd) | Cost per service, container and process manager app |
| [AI coding tools](/providers/llm) | OpenTelemetry from the tool | Plan cost, usage value, sessions and seat fit |

More sources are on the way. If you need one that is not listed, contact [support@costoptix.com](mailto:support@costoptix.com).

## Who it is for

- **Engineering teams** who want to know which service, workload or session is driving spend, and what to fix first.
- **Finance teams** who need budgets, forecasts and per-team bills that match the invoice.
- **Leaders** who want one number for the whole estate and a clear trend.
