## 1. Set up your organisation

Sign in to your own organisation as an admin and open **Customers**. **Your setup** at the top ticks these off as you go.

| Do this | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Invite the rest of your team | **Organization Admin → Users → Invite User** | Only partner **admins** can manage customers. Members and viewers can't. |
| Add the services you charge for | **Settings → Services** | Every customer on Growth and above uses this catalogue and its rates. See [Catalogue Services](/providers/services). |
| Set your bill format | **Settings → Provider bills → Bill format** | Set the columns of your monthly bill once and every customer uses them. See [Provider Bills](/providers/provider-bills#set-the-bill-format). |

Rates start on a date and never change past days, so add each service with the date its price begins.

## 2. Add each customer

Open **Customers** in the main menu and choose **Add customer**. Four short steps:

1. **Customer.** Its name and plan. The list stops at your highest plan. Services and provider bills need **Growth** or above.
2. **Currency.** Amounts are never converted, so host rates, services and provider bills must all be in this currency. Connecting AWS, Azure or GCP needs a USD organisation, and Kubernetes costs show in USD. It can't change once costs arrive.
3. **Their admin.** The email of the person who looks after costs on their side. They get an invite to sign in as admin and invite the rest of their team. Skip it to invite them later from the customer's row.
4. **Check** and create.

**Add customer** is turned off once you reach your customer limit.

## 3. Set up each customer

Each customer shows its progress, for example **2 of 5 set up · Next: Connect what they run**. Choose **Continue setup** to go inside the customer, straight to that step. A banner shows you are inside, and everything you do is in its [audit log](/account/audit-log). Its **Next** button takes you to the following step.

The steps:

1. **Currency**, if you didn't pick it when adding the customer. In **Settings**.
2. **Connect what they run:**
   - [Hosts](/providers/hosts): install the host agent on each machine and set what each one costs.
   - [Kubernetes](/providers/kubernetes) clusters.
   - [AWS](/providers/aws), [Azure](/providers/azure) or [GCP](/providers/gcp) accounts, for a USD customer.
   - [LLM](/providers/llm) coding tools.
3. **Teams.** Create the customer's teams on [Ownership](/features/ownership) and claim what each one owns, so bills split by team.
4. **Services**, for Growth and above. Under **Settings → Services**, record what the customer uses, with the team for each line. If your catalogue is still empty, you can add services on the same page. They go into your catalogue, shared with all your customers.
5. **First admin**, if you skipped it. On the Customers page choose **More** on the customer, then **Do this** next to **Invite their first admin**. You don't need to go inside the customer.

Choose **Back to** your organisation in the banner when you're done.

Agent keys you create inside the customer keep working if you later leave the partner. Other API keys you create there stop working once you can no longer open the customer.

## 4. Every month

After each month ends:

1. Wait for the month to close. Host bills seal about two days after month end, and Services bills five days after.
2. Open the customer, or let its admins do it, and go to **Settings → Provider bills**.
3. **Import a bill**: pick the month, choose the bill's CSV and, if you like, enter the invoice total as a check.
4. Under **Bill lines**, match anything that wasn't matched to a host or service. Later bills reuse your matches.
5. Check the month on **Ownership → Monthly bills**. It shows **Reconciled to provider bill**, and team totals add up to your bill.

A bill imported before the month closes waits and reconciles on its own. From the 6th, customers whose bill for last month is missing show under **Needs attention** on the Customers page. See [Provider Bills](/providers/provider-bills) for how lines are matched and split.

- [ ] Your team invited as admins
- [ ] Service catalogue added with start dates
- [ ] Bill format saved
- [ ] For each customer: created, currency set, sources connected, teams made, services recorded, first admin invited
- [ ] First month: bill imported, lines matched, Ownership shows reconciled
