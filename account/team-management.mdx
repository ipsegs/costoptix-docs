<Note>
  This page is about people who can sign in to CostOptix. To assign spend to engineering teams, see [Ownership](/features/ownership).
</Note>

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| `admin` | Everything: sources, users, billing, webhooks, SSO, budgets, teams and all cost data |
| `member` | View cost data, dashboards, anomalies, recommendations and ownership. Cannot change organisation settings, budgets or users. |

The person who creates the organisation is an admin.

---

## User limits

| Plan | Users |
|---|---|
| Starter | 1 |
| Growth | 3 |
| Professional | 10 |
| Business | 50 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

---

## Inviting people

Open **Organization Admin** from the profile menu, go to **Users** and click **Invite User**. Enter their email and pick a role. They get an email with a link that expires after a while. Pending invitations are on the **Invitations** tab.

With [SSO](/account/sso) turned on, people from your email domain are created automatically the first time they sign in, so you do not need to invite them.

---

## Changing a role

In **Organization Admin → Users**, open the person and click **Edit User**. A role change applies from their next request.

---

## Removing access

In **Organization Admin → Users**, open the person, click **Edit User** and untick **Account is active**. They can no longer sign in, and their history is kept. You cannot deactivate yourself.
